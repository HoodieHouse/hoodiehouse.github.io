Heartfield — Delete Your Play Stats

Heartfield is a farming game made by HoodieHouse. It has no accounts, and we don't collect your personal information. The only data we hold from the game is its anonymous play stats (how the farm is played), filed under your farm's random id. You can delete them here, right away.

How to delete them

  1. In Heartfield, open ☰ → Settings.
  2. Under Share play stats, tap Delete my play stats. This page opens with your farm's id already filled in. (Or type in the id shown there, like 0123-4567-89ab-cdef.)
  3. Tap Delete below.

What is deleted, and what is kept

  • Deleted at once: every play stat sent from that farm.
  • Kept: only a note of the farm's random id and the date of your request, so that nothing more from that farm is ever kept. Nothing else.

Data we never had

  • Your farm (save game) stays on your phone and in your own backups. We never receive it. Delete the app to remove it. On Android, also delete the game's backup in Settings → Google → Backup. On iPhone, delete its iCloud copy in Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage.
  • Ads are shown by Google AdMob; for data Google holds, see https://myadcenter.google.com.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple or Google; we never see your payment details.
